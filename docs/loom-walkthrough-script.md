# Portfolio walkthrough: Loom script (about 2.5 minutes)

Speak naturally; the words in **bold** are what to stress. [Brackets] are what to show on screen.
Aim for ~150 words a minute. Don't read it word for word: glance, then talk.

---

## 0:00 – 0:15 · Hook
[Homepage, your face in the Loom bubble]

"Hi, I'm Harsh. I'm a product manager with five years in EdTech and B2B SaaS, working on **data platforms, growth and applied AI**. In the next two minutes I'll show you what I've built, and how I think, with real numbers."

## 0:15 – 0:50 · The day job
[Click Work → Lead management platform. Scroll to the metrics.]

"At KollegeApply I was the sole PM on our lead platform. When I joined, about fifty people ran lead distribution on spreadsheets. We turned it into a configuration-led platform that processed **78 million records** for **600-plus clients**.

[Back → Content & growth platform]

I also led a 0-to-1 content platform: content processing went from **four hours to ten minutes**, and indexed pages doubled from **150K to over 300K**."

## 0:50 – 1:35 · What I build on my own
[Products page, then open the AniLumi case study and scroll to the GA4 country table.]

"Outside work I've shipped **seven live products** to practise the full product loop myself. They're small on purpose. They're proof of method, not proof of scale.

The best example is AniLumi, an anime site. I built it for global fans. But when I cleaned the analytics, I found **22% of my users were bots**, and the real audience was phone users in India, Pakistan and Bangladesh looking for **Hindi dubs**. Bing searches said the same thing. So I changed the roadmap within days.

[Scroll to the email sign-ups chart]

Since then email sign-ups have grown from **3 in the first week to 22 in three days**. And I found a leak: most people never confirmed their email, so that's the next fix, not more forms."

## 1:35 – 2:05 · Applied AI
[Open the RankFlowHQ case study, scroll to the pipeline diagram.]

"On the AI side, RankFlowHQ turns a keyword into a researched, cited article. It's a **pipeline, not a chat box**: research first, grounded drafting, a fallback chain across seven models, and a human review step. I also wrote about how I evaluate LLM features before they ship."

## 2:05 – 2:30 · How I work, and close
[Open How I work. Scroll slowly through the eight steps, then the "Hiring for a specific role?" cards.]

"Everything comes back to one playbook: start where the time disappears, score every bet the same way, clean the numbers before trusting them, and let users rewrite the roadmap. Each step links to where it actually happened.

If you're hiring for a data platform, growth or AI PM role, this page shows you the three things to read first.

I'm **open to PM and AI PM roles**. My email and WhatsApp are on the site. Thanks for watching."
