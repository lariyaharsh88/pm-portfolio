This file is the pattern for a case study. Nothing in it is a real engagement. Copy it, swap the frontmatter, and set `status` to `published` when the write-up is ready. Drafts stay off the work index.

## Context

Open with the workflow, not the solution. Name the number that was wrong and the decision the product had to change.

<Callout type="insight">
An insight is a fact that changed the roadmap. One or two sentences. The recruiter should be able to repeat it.
</Callout>

<Metric value="10M+" label="Records in the system you owned" />

## Decision

Put the options you actually weighed side by side. Mark the one you shipped.

<Options>
  <Option title="Rewrite the platform">
    A full rebuild would have touched every workflow at once. It also would have hidden whether any single change was responsible for the result.
  </Option>
  <Option title="Ship a thin slice" chosen>
    A narrow loop had an owner, a metric, and a way to turn it off. That was the option that shipped.
  </Option>
</Options>

<Callout type="decision">
A decision names what you chose and what you refused. Keep it specific enough that another PM could argue with it.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tradeoff">
A tradeoff is the cost you accepted. Speed against coverage, quality against spend, or a manual step you left in place on purpose.
</Callout>

## Result

Compare two numbers in the same unit. `5K` and `200K+` are read as thousands. Plain numbers such as `240` and `10` are compared as-is, so a shorter cycle time shows a shorter bar.

<Compare before="5K" after="200K+" label="Monthly sessions" />

<Compare before="240" after="10" label="Minutes to process a batch" />

<Callout type="result">
A result is the number after the change, stated once, with the unit still attached.
</Callout>
